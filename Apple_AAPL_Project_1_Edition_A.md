# Project 1 — Edition A

## Apple Inc. (AAPL)

**Valuation date:** September 1, 2026  
**Investment call:** **WATCH-DEFER**  
**User:** An investor evaluating whether to initiate a position in Apple Inc.  
**Source:** Analyst-supplied decision context.

## Decision

**WATCH-DEFER — Do not initiate a position at this time.**

Apple’s fiscal 2025 results demonstrate strong operating performance, particularly in Services, but the evidence assembled so far is insufficient to conclude that Apple’s expected future cash flows justify its market valuation on September 1, 2026. **Source: Analyst judgment based on the evidence below.**

Apple remains worthy of further consideration because fiscal 2025 Services net sales increased approximately 14% to $109.158 billion and Services generated a 75.4% gross margin. **Source: Apple Inc., 2025 Form 10-K, pp. 21–23 and 30.**

Strong business performance does not necessarily produce an attractive investment when the market price already reflects that performance. **Source: Analyst valuation principle and judgment.**

**What would change the call:** I would change the call to **INITIATE-BUY** if an updated valuation using Apple’s fiscal Q3 2026 information, reasonable cash-flow assumptions, and its September 1, 2026 market price produced a meaningful margin of safety without depending on sustained unusually high Services growth. **Source: Analyst decision rule.**

The filing covered the fiscal year ended September 27, 2025, was filed October 31, 2025, and has SEC accession number 0000320193-25-000079. **Source: [SEC filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/0000320193-25-000079-index.htm).**

## Verified financial evidence

This is a fiscal-year weighted average, not a point-in-time share count on the valuation date. **Source: Description of the measure in Apple Inc., 2025 Form 10-K; analyst clarification.**

Apple reported 14.773 billion shares issued and outstanding at September 27, 2025. **Source: Apple Inc., 2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Balance Sheets.**

Apple subsequently reported 14,594,180,000 shares issued and outstanding as of July 17, 2026. **Source: [Apple Inc., fiscal Q3 2026 Form 10-Q, cover page](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000020/aapl-20260627.htm#ib65f44d2415e4f40854b44fa987f6c2a_67).**

Apple reported $35.934 billion of cash and cash equivalents at September 27, 2025. **Source: Apple Inc., 2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Balance Sheets.**

Apple also held $18.763 billion of current marketable securities and $77.723 billion of non-current marketable securities at that date. **Source: Apple Inc., 2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Balance Sheets and Note 4.**

Whether some or all of those securities should be treated as non-operating assets is an assumption that must be stated explicitly. **Source: Analyst valuation judgment.**

Apple reported $91.281 billion of term-debt principal and $7.979 billion of commercial paper at September 27, 2025, for a combined approximately $99.260 billion. **Source: Apple Inc., 2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Balance Sheets and Note 9; analyst calculation.**

The carrying amount of term debt was $90.678 billion after unamortized discounts, issuance costs, and hedge-accounting adjustments. **Source: Apple Inc., 2025 Form 10-K, Note 9.**

## Operating-value driver

Apple’s fiscal 2025 Services net sales were $109.158 billion, compared with $96.169 billion in fiscal 2024. **Source: Apple Inc., 2025 Form 10-K, net-sales table and Consolidated Statements of Operations.**

The increase was approximately 13.5%, which Apple reported as 14% after rounding. **Source: Apple Inc., 2025 Form 10-K; analyst calculation.**

Services gross margin was 75.4% in fiscal 2025, compared with 73.9% in fiscal 2024. **Source: Apple Inc., 2025 Form 10-K, gross-margin table.**

Products gross margin was 36.8% in fiscal 2025, compared with 37.2% in fiscal 2024. **Source: Apple Inc., 2025 Form 10-K, gross-margin table.**

Because Services grew faster and generated a substantially higher gross margin than Products, its future growth could materially affect Apple’s revenue mix, profitability, and operating value. **Source: Reported figures above and analyst inference.**

The available evidence does not establish that Services can maintain its fiscal 2025 growth rate or margin over a long-term valuation horizon. **Source: Analyst assessment of the evidence set.**

## Consequential assumptions

- Services can continue producing meaningful growth and relatively high margins. **Source: Analyst assumption.**
- Demand for Apple’s products will remain reasonably durable. **Source: Analyst assumption.**
- Apple will continue competing effectively as technology and consumer preferences change. **Source: Analyst assumption.**
- Strong operating results may already be reflected in Apple’s September 1, 2026 market valuation. **Source: Analyst assumption requiring market-price and valuation analysis.**
- Apple’s ecosystem and installed base will continue supporting demand for Products and Services. **Source: Analyst assumption not yet independently tested.**

## Material unknowns

The current evidence does not determine whether Apple’s September 1, 2026 market valuation is justified by expected future cash flows and growth. **Source: Analyst assessment; valuation not yet completed.**

The analysis has not established whether Services can sustain its recent growth and margins. **Source: Analyst assessment of the evidence gap.**

The analysis has not quantified Apple’s continuing dependence on iPhone sales or the cash-flow effect of changing iPhone demand. **Source: Analyst assessment of the evidence gap.**

The effects of emerging technologies and competitors on Apple’s long-term position have not been evaluated. **Source: Analyst assessment of the evidence gap.**

The financial effects of regulation, tariffs, supply-chain exposure, and international risks have not been quantified. **Source: Analyst assessment of the evidence gap.**

## Falsification question

**What if Apple’s Services growth slows materially over the next several years—would Apple still offer an adequate return and margin of safety at its September 1, 2026 market valuation?**

**Source:** Partner-generated falsification question, refined to connect operating performance to valuation.

Evidence that Apple remains attractively valued under materially slower Services growth would weaken **WATCH-DEFER** and could support **INITIATE-BUY**. **Source: Analyst judgment.**

Evidence that the current price requires sustained high Services growth, expanding margins, or other aggressive assumptions would reinforce **WATCH-DEFER** or could support **DO NOT INITIATE**. **Source: Analyst judgment.**
